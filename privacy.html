Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

"Explainer Shorts Uploader" (the app) is a desktop script used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram).

What data the app uses

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens and the account ID (for TikTok: open_id and display name, scope user.info.basic) of the owner's accounts, to upload videos to them.
  • The video files and captions the owner chooses to upload.

How it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file readable only by the owner. Nothing is sent to any server other than the official APIs of the platform being uploaded to.

What the app does not do

The app does not collect data about other users or viewers, does not use analytics or cookies, does not sell or share any data, and is not offered to the public.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services). Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/naxel/explainer-shorts-legal/issues.
